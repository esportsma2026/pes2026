Tournament Rules

Rules for the World Cup 2026 tournament. Follow them to keep the competition fun and fair.

01 🤝

Respect for all group members is mandatory.

02 📜

Every participant must comply with the tournament rules.

Read and follow all regulations set by the organizers.

03 ⚽

All styles of play are allowed.

There is no restricted tactic, formation, or play approach.

04 ⏱️

Boring time-wasting tactics are not allowed.

If a player wastes time for more than 5 minutes, the match is stopped and counted as a forfeit.

05 🕐

Duration of matches.

Each match is played for 11 minutes.

06 ➡️

Directions.

Play follows the direction of the arrows.

07 🥅

Extra time & penalties.

Extra time and penalty shootouts are only available in the knockout rounds.

08 🔌

Disconnection.

If you disconnect twice for any reason, it is considered a forfeit, unless your opponent agrees to replay the match.

09 🏷️

Correct profile.

You must set up your national team correctly in your profile, otherwise the match is forfeited.

10 📮

Sending results.

Results and reports are sent from the website’s ‘Support’ section.

Note: By registering you accept the tournament rules and agree to respect all the participants.
